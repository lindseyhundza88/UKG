Why is there a gap?Bill4Time only captures billable work. Your UKG total includes everything — lunch, brief admin tasks, and billing increment rounding. A gap of 1–2 hours is completely normal and expected.
⚠
Large gaps between Bill4Time and UKG require review. If your billable hours are significantly lower than your UKG clock hours and lunch/admin don't account for the difference, check for missing entries, open timers, or unlogged tasks before the end of the day.
